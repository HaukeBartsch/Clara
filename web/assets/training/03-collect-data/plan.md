# Collect data

Participants are records. Create a record on the **Record Status Dashboard** and fill out its forms. You need to be a member of the project with data entry rights.

1. Open the project and choose **Record Status Dashboard**. Type the new participant's record ID (or take the proposed one) and choose **Create**.
2. The record view opens. In the table at the top — one row per instrument, one column per event — choose the form to fill out. Fill in the fields, set **Completion** and choose **Save**. Hints below a field point out values that look wrong; required fields are listed before saving.
3. A green line confirms that the form was saved. Nothing is stored for a new participant until the first form is saved.
4. Back on the **Record Status Dashboard**, each record is one row and each dot one form at one event. The colour shows the state — grey has no data, blue some data, yellow is unverified, green is finished — and the tiles in full colour how many of the form's fields hold a value. Choose a dot to open that form.

## Tip

Every change is kept with its time and author. In the record view, the ⟲ button next to a field of a saved record opens its **History** with all its earlier values.
