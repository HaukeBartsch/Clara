# Collect data

Participants are records. Create a record on the **Record Status Dashboard** and fill out its forms. You need to be a member of the project with data entry rights.

1. Open the project and choose **Record Status Dashboard**. Type the new participant's record ID (or take the proposed one) and choose **Create**.
2. The record view opens. Choose the event and the instrument, fill in the fields, set **Completion** and choose **Save**. Hints below a field point out values that look wrong; required fields are listed before saving.
3. A green line confirms that the form was saved. Nothing is stored for a new participant until the first form is saved.
4. Back on the **Record Status Dashboard**, each record is one row and each dot one form at one event: grey has no data, yellow some data, green is finished. Choose a dot to open that form.

## Tip

Every change is kept with its time and author. In the record view, the history of a field shows all its earlier values.
